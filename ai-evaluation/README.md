# Indonesian response evaluation — practice starter

This folder contains original, fictional exercises for learning to explain evaluation decisions. It is not an employer test, a confidential rubric or evidence of paid evaluation experience. The worked examples are reference annotations; they should not be submitted as independent assessment answers.

## Rubric

Score each dimension from 1 to 3, then explain the most important issue in plain language.

| Dimension | 1 — poor | 2 — mixed | 3 — strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instruction following | Misses a required condition | Meets some conditions | Meets all explicit conditions |
| Factual grounding | Contradicts the supplied facts | Adds uncertain or unsupported detail | Stays within the supplied evidence |
| Indonesian clarity | Hard to understand | Understandable but awkward | Natural, specific and concise |
| Usefulness | Does not help complete the task | Helps partly | Gives a clear, relevant next step |

Do not hide an unknown fact behind a confident score. Identify missing evidence. If two responses have different strengths, explain the tradeoff rather than forcing a vague “better” judgment.

## Worked example 1: grounded rewrite

**Source facts:** registration closes Friday, 15:00 WIB; submit through the school form; no registration fee.  
**Task:** write a short Indonesian reminder without adding details.

**Response A:** “Pendaftaran ditutup Jumat pukul 15.00 WIB. Silakan isi formulir sekolah sebelum batas waktu. Tidak ada biaya pendaftaran.”

**Response B:** “Buruan daftar sekarang! Kuota tinggal 10 orang. Pendaftaran gratis sampai Jumat malam.”

**Reference judgment:** prefer A. It preserves the deadline, action and cost. B invents a remaining quota and changes 15:00 WIB to “malam”. A scores 3/3/3/3; B scores 1/1/2/2. The factual contradiction matters more than B's energetic tone.

## Worked example 2: following a numerical constraint

**Task:** “Sebutkan tepat dua langkah pertama saat laptop tidak tersambung ke Wi-Fi. Gunakan bahasa sederhana.”

**Response A:** “1. Pastikan Wi-Fi menyala dan mode pesawat mati. 2. Coba sambungkan kembali ke jaringan yang benar.”

**Response B:** “Restart laptop, instal ulang Windows, reset router, ganti kartu Wi-Fi.”

**Reference judgment:** prefer A. It provides exactly two low-impact checks in clear language. B gives four actions and jumps to disruptive steps without evidence. This is a practice judgment, not a device diagnosis.

## Your independent practice

Write three additional prompts, evaluate two responses for each, and record your own rationale before reading any reference answer. Keep a column for uncertainty and the evidence used. Do not use AI assistance in a hiring test when the employer prohibits it.

A future standalone repository could be named `indonesian-ai-evaluation-portfolio`; start it when you have independently reviewed examples you can defend in an interview.
